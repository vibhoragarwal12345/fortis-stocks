# Exchange screensaver — image credits (legacy, may be removed)

Black-and-white dashboard backdrop. Only NYSE / NASDAQ buildings. All from
Wikimedia Commons; rendered B&W + graded in-app. Each has a small `-fill.webp`
companion used only for the blurred ambient fill.

- **sv-nyse-building** — "Exterior of Stock Exchange, New York City" (LCCN95501303).
  Public domain (published pre-1931).
- **sv-nyse-facade** — New York Stock Exchange, Manhattan, by Ken Lund, 2012.
  CC BY-SA 2.0.
- **sv-nyse-corner** — New York Stock Exchange, June 2024. CC BY 4.0.
- **sv-nyse-angle** — New York Stock Exchange, December 2022. CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **sv-nasdaq** — Nasdaq Tower (MarketSite, Times Square), May 2026. CC BY 4.0.
- **sv-nyse-flags** — "5.2.11 New York Stock Exchange" by Luigi Novi, 2011.
  CC BY-SA 3.0.

Attribution is surfaced in-app via the site footer credits line. CC BY-SA images
require derivatives under a compatible license. To expand the pool (target 10-15),
add more NYSE/NASDAQ/Wall Street shots here with matching `-fill` companions.
